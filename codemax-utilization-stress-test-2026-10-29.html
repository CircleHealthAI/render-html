Utilization page stress test: CodeMax - Lumina

Org 38, October 2026, read-only, 2026-10-29. Code: circle-v2 82ebb7f5d.

The arithmetic is correct. Six rules count the wrong things. Index, facility pages, export and range splits all agree. An independent recount from raw Kipu notes (listForPatient) matched 1,281 of 1,284 patient rows. The other 3 were notes synced after the last summary refresh.

HIGH1. Individual Encounters counts every billable Kipu evaluation

Evidence: 30,409 of 60,800 documented services are individual encounters. Only 5,682 have a session time. By title, 8,046 are nurse notes and 12,322 are scales, screens or admin forms (GAD-7, PHQ-9, CIWA, problem list, drug screen consent, belongings search, incident report). Uprise Treatment Center: 98 per patient in 29 days.

Cause: kipuIndividualEncounterRowPredicate accepts any evaluation with billable = true. CodeMax turns that toggle on for nursing and admin forms. billing_codes is empty on every form, including therapy notes.

Fix: Require the evaluation_start_and_end_time item (see recordsKipuEvaluationEnd). In a 60-document sample it separated every session note from the rest. Add a Dictionary override for the exceptions (one PHQ-9 form has start/end, one therapy note form does not). Result: about 36,100 services instead of 60,800.

HIGH2. Auth days wrong on 364 of 1,088 rows

Evidence: Production sums every review at the latest level of care, whatever its status. 142 "Not Required" rows show 6,034 days, and 172 rows with only a "New" request show 4,030.

Fix: #2442 (DEV-3476), merged 11:04 AM ET. It was not deployed at 11:40 AM. Recheck after the deploy.

Cause: Kipu form times have no zone and use the Pacific account clock. Circle reads them in the facility zone (_integrations/Kipu.md #23). Group times carry an offset and are correct. Example at Serene: an evaluation typed as 2:56 PM is read as 2:56 PM ET and overlaps a 3:00 to 5:00 PM ET group. The note was really at 5:56 PM ET.

Fix: Read typed times in the offset of the document's created_at. Then run backfill-utilization-services.ts, because utilization_services stores start times at sync time.

MEDIUM4. The program filter counts services twice

Evidence: The Singer Island program views sum to 94 more services than "All programs" (Clean Treatment +69). One patient has 73 services under Detox and 70 under Residential 2.

Cause: serviceHeldInProgramPredicate accepts a stay from any of the patient's episodes. The patient's earlier episode has a Detox stay that started 9/25 and has no end date.

Fix: Match on program_stays.treatment_episode_id = utilization_services.treatment_episode_id. End an open stay at the next stay's start or at discharge.

MEDIUM5. Stale facilities show 0

29 facilities show 0 services for 201 active patients. Their last Kipu sync was June to September (for example, ABC Wellness IOP: 19 active, last sync 9/10). Fix: show "No data since <date>" instead of 0.

LOW6. Export hours do not add up to the page total

Rows are rounded before export, so NoHo's rows sum to 5,203.6 h against 5,202.0 h on the page. Export minutes, or label the rounding.

Not bugs: service-type day rows sum to more than the total (one day can hold both types), and the sync lag is about 15 minutes. Limits: the reference data is Circle's synced copy of Kipu, and auth "used" days were not rebuilt independently. Reads used: utilization.listFacilities, orgLedger, facilityLedger, listForPatient, episodeAuthorizations, patientCharts.getById, programStays.listForPatient.
