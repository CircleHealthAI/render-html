UR Voicemail Script — Feedback Review

Topic: Your UR specialist's feedback on the SUD voicemail script · what we verified, what we rebuilt, and what we need from you

1We verified every issue against the charts

  • We took the feedback email and the three uploaded chart PDFs and checked each issue page by page.
  • Everything flagged checked out — the PAWS symptoms, the craving scale, and the medication refusal are all clearly documented in the chart and the review missed them.
  • The craving scale was actually documented in three places: the ASAM form, the nursing daily check-ins, and the doctor's note.

2We found the two root causes

  • Stale data: the review built its picture almost entirely from the June 1 paperwork and ignored the June 4–5 notes — so it reported "minimal anxiety" when the patient rated it 9/10 three days later. The newer, more severe data is what actually supports medical necessity.
  • ASAM edition mismatch: the facility charts on the new ASAM 4th edition, where "Dimension 4" means substance-use risks — that's where the PAWS checklist and craving scale live. The old script asked in 3rd-edition language, so the AI was looking under the wrong headings.

3What the rebuilt script does now

  • Follows the ASAM 4th edition exactly like your charts, section for section.
  • Dates every data point, always uses the newest note for current status, and shows trends across the week instead of one snapshot.
  • Reads as an actual word-for-word script, ending with the explicit ask: level of care, days requested, discharge date, step-down plan.

4Medication handling, fully reworked

  • PRNs listed separately with what each one is for.
  • Dose increases and decreases recognized (discontinued + restarted at a new strength).
  • Monthly injectables like Sublocade counted as active MAT even when the order shows "expired."
  • Correct anti-craving definitions — no more labeling Seroquel or Vistaril as anti-craving meds.
  • Refusals reported front and center — a documented refusal actually strengthens the case for 24-hour care.

5The payer-specific detail you asked for

  • Named triggers, named coping skills — specifics, not generalities.
  • Your a-through-f coping-skills independence scale, word for word, with the supporting evidence.

6Your "ask me when something's off" idea

  • Every script now ends with a "Discrepancies & Questions for UR Review" section just for you — not read to the payer.
  • It lists chart contradictions, missing info (SI/HI updates, sleep, appetite), and odd values to verify before you dial.
  • Example from this chart: "age 6" for first alcohol use is genuinely in the chart — that's a verify-with-clinical flag, not an AI error.

7What we need from you

  • Include the MAR in uploads. The current med-list export shows order dates only, so "active PRNs in the last 48 hours" can't be computed without the administration record.
  • Payer check: would any of your payers push back on 4th-edition framing? If so, we'll clone a 3rd-edition variant rather than water this one down.
